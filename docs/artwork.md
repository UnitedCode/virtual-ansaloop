## Base prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: photoreal base image for a working hardware prototype interface. Edit target: the attached actual Panasonic EASA-PHONE KX-T1421 photograph. Straighten the device with a slight camera rotation and gentle perspective correction: grille slats and front control row should be horizontal, device centered and nearly overhead, front controls at bottom, with a small realistic amount of depth. Preserve this EXACT model and its original proportions, brown woodgrain ribbed speaker grille across the top, Panasonic wordmark, large dark transparent cassette lid, TWO separate vertical cassette wells, cream front strip, LEFT THREE-POSITION SLIDE SWITCH, existing front keys, and the original tiny indicator lenses (left messages light, record light, play/start-stop light, power light). Keep every physical key and indicator in its original relative position; do not simplify or add controls, no redesigned faceplate. Remove only the two tapes from their wells so that accurately sized photoreal cassette overlays can be inserted later; retain the original cassette well structures beneath the clear dark lid. Preserve original printing from the photo faithfully, including Panasonic, EASA-PHONE, MICROPROCESSOR CONTROL, TONE REMOTE CONTROL, RECORD, REVIEW, START/STOP, CUE, POWER/IND. Clean isolated cutout on a fully transparent background, with no cable, adapter, tabletop, text banners, UI, extra labels or watermark. Frame the entire machine with a narrow even transparent margin. Neutral natural product photography; this is a faithful correction of the source photo, not a new product design. Do not invent unseen rear connectors or side controls.

Reference image 1 is the original KX-T1421 photograph; use the actual Panasonic ENDLESS outgoing-message tape visible in its right cassette well as the design reference. Reference image 2 is the corrected straightened machine base; match its lighting, age, plastic texture and nearly overhead camera. Produce ONLY one compact ENDLESS LOOP cassette, flat face up, long edge HORIZONTAL, standard approximately 1.58:1 cassette-body proportions, centered with a very narrow even transparent margin. Dark translucent charcoal plastic body, accurately detailed two tape spools and metal spindle hubs, the understated aged cream Panasonic paper label with a thin brown/orange stripe from the reference. Exact modest label text: Panasonic, ENDLESS. Leave a small blank label area at the lower right for an interface to add the loop length; do not print any duration. It must look like an actual small 1980s tape cassette photographed with the same camera, not a UI icon or illustration. Entire outer background genuinely transparent. No shadows outside the cassette, no surrounding device, no cables, no extra objects, no banners, no explanatory copy, no fictional branding. Preserve the source's restrained real material colors and proportions; the aim is a faithful insert asset that fits the machine's cassette wells.
