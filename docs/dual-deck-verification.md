# Dual decks and physical LED module — verification

Date: 2026-11-22 (America/Chicago). Updates were built and tested locally;
the existing hosted Site was not updated.

## Firmware and audio

- One ARM image controls two GPIO transport groups and two SAI audio channels.
  Rebuilding tracked firmware reproduces SHA256
  `03f0fcd226c6406124c5cfc43690a3d5ea4b014ff87d52c9178a4b852895c497`.
- All ten native engine integration tests passed. Three new tests cover
  simultaneous recording/playback of distinct 0.75/1.25-second loops,
  independent Stop and swapping, independent source EOF, and separate media
  libraries with equal-length tapes. Both loop autocorrelations exceed 0.999;
  cross-channel correlation is below 0.05. SAI underrun/overrun flags stay clear.
- The original seven tests continue to pass, including microphone PCM,
  corruption rejection, exact packet-boundary invariance, 6/8-second media,
  and a 120.5-second tape restore/export.
- Three worklet PCM boundary tests, native/WASM strict Clippy, formatting and
  the WASM build passed. The layout's component/pad/mount clearance test passed.

## Browser

The test tab used localhost:3030; the user's original 127.0.0.1 tab was retained.
The earlier right-deck library migrated without renaming/deleting its records;
left-deck records have a separate key namespace.

- Recorded different local files onto a right 1.5-second and left 2.25-second
  tape. Both simultaneously reached recording GPIO 15 with independent writes.
- Stopped/exported the left while the right continued recording, then played
  the left while the right recorded. Both subsequently played together at GPIO
  3; observed channel RMS levels were 0.2161 left and 0.2148 right.
- Stopping both, power cycling and reloading restored both inserted lengths.
  WAVs before and after reload had identical hashes for each deck:
  - Right: `3b45836492eb48b6be3affbf66b099f17937767ec98c85749d7cbac1b19e8024`
  - Left: `8b074d85321c34dfe0a47b168ed51af2f3f03c3cc0dc617ebda40e3449d0acf5`
- Checked the cassette duration labels independently, selection and transport
  controls, and the raised bezel / PCB overlay on the narrower browser layout.

No subjective listening acceptance or actual microphone capture was established.
The live carrier remains synthetic and has not been calibrated to the Panasonic.
The dual image has not been run through the separate native ngspice coordinator.

## Mechanical preview

The decorative strip was replaced by millimeter-based SVG assets generated by
Rust. The 150 × 24 × 14 mm module plus 1 mm mounting pad includes a 144 × 18 mm
PCB, real LED/package envelopes, reverse-side driver and bypass/current-setting
parts, a six-pin header, internal fasteners and an external hinge-harness proposal.
The dimensioned top/section drawing and PCB toggle were inspected in the browser.

This verifies component space in the proposal, not routing/manufacturability or
fit on a real lid. Exact KX-T1421 case dimensions and hinge/internal clearance
could not be verified from the sources found. See sequencer-fit.md for sources,
the cassette-based photo scale and the checks still needed before fabrication.
